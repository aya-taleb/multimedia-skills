# YouTube facts these skills rely on

Checked on 2026-10-23 against YouTube Help. If a page says something different today, the page
wins. Re-check before quoting a limit to a user.

| fact | source |
| --- | --- |
| Titles: up to 100 characters. Descriptions: up to 5,000 characters. | https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/57407 |
| Tags "play a minimal role in your video's discovery" unless the content is often misspelled. Title, thumbnail and description matter more. Excessive tags in the description break the spam policy. | https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402 |
| Manual chapters: first timestamp 00:00, at least three timestamps in ascending order, each chapter at least 10 seconds, each with a title, listed in the description. | https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579 |
| Shorts: square or vertical videos up to three minutes (uploads from Oct 15, 2024). A Short over one minute with an active copyright claim is blocked. | https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15424877 |
| "Half of all channels and videos on YouTube have an impressions CTR that can range between 2% and 10%." CTR tends to fall when a video gets many impressions, for example from the Home page. | https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7628154 |
| Studio Analytics, Advanced mode: "Export current view" downloads a report, limited to 500 rows. | https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9717005 |

Things we do NOT have, so the skills never state them as fact:

- Search volume for a phrase. YouTube does not publish it. Use the search bar's suggestions and
  the Research tab in YouTube Studio as signs of demand, and say they are signs, not numbers.
- How many characters of a title show in search on a given device. It varies. Put the main
  words in the first 40 to 50 characters as a safe habit, and say it is a habit, not a rule.
- Any "good" retention or view duration benchmark. Compare a channel with its own median.
