# Job spec: youtube-seo

Written before reading any other YouTube SEO skill.

## The job

A founder is about to publish (or wants to fix) a YouTube video. They need the words around
the video that help the right people find it: a title that matches what people search and
promises what the video delivers, a description whose first lines work in search results,
chapters, and a few tags. They also need to know what not to bother with.

## Inputs

- The transcript or a summary of the video, the business file, and the audience.
- Optional: search terms they already know people use, the current title and description.

## Output

- 3 title options with the main search phrase early, each under the visible length, plus why.
- A description: first 2 lines carry the promise and the phrase; then what is in the video,
  chapters (if the video has clear sections), links the founder gives, no invented links.
- Up to about 10 tags, noting tags matter little.
- A short "how to check demand" note: YouTube search suggestions and YouTube Studio's Research
  tab, done by the founder, since the skill has no search volume data.
- Written to `media/youtube/<slug>-seo.md`.

## What good looks like

- Honest about what it cannot know (search volume, ranking).
- Title matches the content; no bait the video does not pay off.
- Uses the founder's tone and words.

## Must not

- Invent search volumes, rankings or "SEO scores".
- Keyword-stuff.
- Log in to YouTube or edit the video for the user.
