# Harbor Wick Candle Co.

- Name: Harbor Wick Candle Co.
- One-line pitch: Small-batch soy candles poured by hand in a shop near the ferry.
- Who it serves: locals and weekend visitors who want a gift that smells like the coast
- Stage: open 3 years, sells in the shop and online
- Location: Port Townsend, WA

## Fall scents
- Cedar Smoke (amber glass jar)
- Pear and Clove (white ceramic tin)
- Low Tide (clear glass jar, sea salt label)
- Apple Orchard (amber glass jar)

## Tone
- Tone words: warm, plain, unhurried
- Words to avoid: luxe, curated, elevated

## Brand look
- Colors: oat, charcoal, a little rust
- Feel: natural light, real textures, nothing glossy

## Off-limits
- awards, celebrity mentions, price claims
