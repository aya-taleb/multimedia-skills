- One-line pitch: Shared to-do lists for small agency teams that show who is stuck.

- Stage: beta, 212 teams
- Goals: 50 paying teams by June
- Topics: running a small agency, async work, building in public
- Tone: direct, warm, a bit dry
- Words to avoid: hustle, crush it, ninja, rockstar
- Proof I can use: 212 beta teams; beta teams report 3.5 hours a week saved on status meetings (from our March survey)
- Off-limits: client names, revenue numbers
