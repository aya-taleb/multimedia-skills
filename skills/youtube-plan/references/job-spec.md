# Job spec: youtube-plan

Written before reading any other YouTube planning skill.

## The job

A founder with limited time wants a YouTube plan they can actually keep: what to make for the
next 4 to 8 weeks, in what order, at what pace, and how each video ties back to the business.
Most founders overplan and stop. The plan has to fit their real hours.

## Inputs

- The business file (who they serve, goals, topics, proof).
- Hours per week they can give, their format (talking head, screen recording, podcast clips),
  and any videos already made or ideas they have.

## Output

- A pace that fits the hours (with the time math shown), a calendar of videos with working
  titles, the viewer question each answers, the format, and the next step it leads to
  (product, email list, consult).
- A mix: mostly searchable "how to" videos for their buyer, a few that show their point of
  view, and Shorts cut from the long videos.
- What to measure after 4 weeks and what would change the plan.
- Written to `media/youtube/plan-<yyyy-mm>.md`.

## What good looks like

- Realistic: fewer, better videos beat a plan they drop in week two.
- Every idea comes from their topics and buyer, not generic trends.
- No promised view counts or growth numbers.

## Must not

- Promise results or invent benchmarks.
- Schedule or upload anything on YouTube.
