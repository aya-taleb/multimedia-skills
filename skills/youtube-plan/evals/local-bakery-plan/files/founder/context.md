# Rise Bakery

- Name: Rise Bakery
- One-line pitch: Neighborhood sourdough bakery, open 7am to 2pm.
- Who it serves: people who live and work within a few blocks; home bakers who want to learn
- Location: Kirkland, WA
- Goals: fill the new Saturday sourdough class ($45, 8 people)

## Tone
- Tone words: warm, plain, neighborly
- Words to avoid: artisanal, elevated
